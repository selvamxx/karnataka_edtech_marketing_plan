Karnataka EdTech · Solo Marketer Plan

3-Month Digital Marketing Plan
April – June 2026

A practical, solo-executable plan to generate school leads, increase demo bookings, and support your sales team — focused only on Karnataka.

Budget: ₹45K–60K Target: School Owners & Principals Language: Kannada + English One Marketer Only
What You Will Do

Four core activities you'll run — content, ads, lead handling, and sales coordination. Simple and focused.

1 · Content (What to Post & How Often)
Kannada content is your biggest advantage. Use it aggressively.
School owners and principals in Karnataka respond much better to content in their own language. Aim for 70% Kannada posts and 30% English posts on all social channels.
Facebook & Instagram 3 posts per week
Post 1 (Monday): School success story or principal quote — in Kannada. Example: "ನಮ್ಮ ಸ್ಕೂಲ್‌ನಲ್ಲಿ Smart Classroom ಬಳಿಕ ಮಕ್ಕಳ ಆಸಕ್ತಿ ಹೆಚ್ಚಿದೆ" (Since Smart Classroom in our school, children's interest has increased).
Post 2 (Wednesday): Product benefit in simple language. Show what a Smart Classroom looks like, what VR Lab does. Use video reels (30–60 seconds).
Post 3 (Friday): Tips for school principals — "5 ways to improve student attendance" or education news relevant to Karnataka schools. Builds trust.
Use Canva (free version) to design posts. You only need 3 templates — story, image post, and reel thumbnail.
WhatsApp & WhatsApp Broadcast 1–2 messages per week
Create one WhatsApp Broadcast list for warm leads (schools that showed interest). Send a short message + 1 image/video every week. Keep it under 3 lines.
Share demo booking link directly in WhatsApp. This is your main conversion channel in Karnataka.
Do NOT send broadcast to cold contacts. Only to people who have already shown interest. WhatsApp works best for warm leads in Karnataka B2B.
YouTube / Reels 2 videos per month
1 school testimonial video per month (film at an existing school, 1–2 minutes). Ask the principal or teacher to speak in Kannada. Add subtitles in English.
1 product demo or walkthrough (Smart Classroom or VR Lab). Keep it simple — mobile camera is fine.
2 · Ads (Meta + Google)
Meta Lead Ads (Facebook/Instagram): Run lead generation ads targeting principals and school management in Karnataka. The ad form collects name, phone, school name automatically — no landing page needed.
Google Search Ads: Target people searching for "smart classroom Karnataka", "interactive panel for schools", "VR lab school Bangalore". These are warm leads — they are already looking for you.
Run only 1 Meta campaign + 1 Google campaign at a time. Do not spread budget across multiple campaigns. Focus is key.
3 · Lead Handling (WhatsApp Focus)
Within 30 minutes: When a new Meta lead comes in, send a WhatsApp message immediately. Don't wait. Speed matters in lead conversion.
Simple script: "ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ [Name] ಅವರೇ, ನಾವು [Company] ನಿಂದ ಮಾತನಾಡುತ್ತಿದ್ದೇವೆ. ನಿಮ್ಮ ಶಾಲೆಗೆ Smart Classroom demo ತೋರಿಸಲು ಇಷ್ಟಪಡುತ್ತೇವೆ. ಯಾವ ದಿನ ಸರಿಯಾಗುತ್ತದೆ?" (When is a good day for a demo?)
Track leads: Use a simple Google Sheet. Columns: Name, School, Phone, Source, Date, Status (New / Contacted / Demo Booked / Closed).
4 · Coordination with Sales Team
Every Monday: Share new leads from the week with the sales team. Send the Google Sheet link or WhatsApp message with new lead names + phone numbers.
Every Friday: Ask the sales team for feedback — which leads became demos, which deals closed. This helps you improve ads.
Ask sales for content: Request 1 school story or 1 principal quote per month. Sales team already visits schools — they can collect this easily.
You don't need complex CRM. A shared Google Sheet works well for a small team. Keep it updated daily.
Month-by-Month Plan

Each month has 4–5 tasks only. Do not add more. Focus wins over volume.

Month 1

Build the Foundation

Set things up right so Months 2 & 3 run smoothly.

Set up Meta Business Manager — Create Facebook/Instagram business page for school-focused ads if not done. Link WhatsApp Business number.
Launch 1 Meta lead ad — Karnataka targeting only. School owners / principals age 30–55. Run in Kannada. Budget: ₹8,000.
Create lead tracking Google Sheet — Share with sales team. Start tracking all incoming leads from Day 1.
Start content calendar — Plan 12 posts (3 per week). Create templates in Canva. Batch-create posts on Sundays.
Collect 2 school testimonials — Coordinate with sales team to get principal quotes or a quick video from existing happy schools.
Expected by end of Month 1: 40–60 leads, 8–12 demo bookings, ads running and optimised, WhatsApp follow-up process working.
Month 2

Improve & Scale

Use data from Month 1 to improve. Add Google Ads now.

Launch Google Search Ads — Target Karnataka-specific keywords like "smart classroom Bangalore", "interactive whiteboard school Karnataka". Budget: ₹7,000.
Optimise Meta ad — Stop the ad set that is performing poorly. Put full budget on the best-performing audience from Month 1.
Publish 2 testimonial posts — Use principal quotes collected in Month 1. Post with school photo. Boost the best one with ₹1,500 extra budget.
WhatsApp nurture sequence — Build a simple 4-message flow for leads who didn't book a demo in Month 1. Send one message every 4 days.
Expected by end of Month 2: 60–80 leads total (cumulative), 20–28 demo bookings, 3–5 deals in pipeline for sales.
Month 3

Push for Demos & Closures

Focus on demo conversion. Support sales with urgency.

Run a limited-time offer ad — "Book a free demo before 30 June and get [special benefit]." Create urgency for school owners sitting on fence.
Retarget warm leads — Run a Meta retargeting campaign to people who clicked on your ad but did not submit the form. Small budget: ₹3,000.
Publish case study content — 1 detailed school success post ("How XYZ School in Dharwad improved results with Smart Classroom"). Works well for principals who need proof.
Review and report — Prepare a simple 1-page summary for management: leads generated, demos done, deals in pipeline, cost per lead.
Expected by end of Month 3: 80–120 total leads, 30–40 demos supported, 5–10 deals expected to close by sales team.
Important: Academic Calendar Alignment
In Karnataka, school decisions for next academic year are made between March and June. This is the best window to generate leads. Do NOT wait until July — the urgency of new academic year preparation works in your favour.
Weekly Work Routine

A simple daily and weekly routine you can follow without burning out. Everything is planned in advance on Sunday.

Sunday (30–45 minutes) — Plan your week
Review the Google Sheet — how many new leads, follow-up status. Write 2–3 posts for the week in Canva. Schedule them using Meta Business Suite (free). Check ad spend from last week.
Monday
Check new leads from weekend. Send WhatsApp to all new leads within 2 hours.
Share new leads with sales team on WhatsApp group.
Post Monday content (school success story in Kannada).
Tuesday
Check Meta Ads Manager — Cost per lead, ad reach, impressions. Note what's working.
Follow up (call or WhatsApp) with leads from last week who didn't respond.
Wednesday
Post Wednesday content (product demo or benefit video).
Reply to all comments and DMs on Facebook/Instagram (20–30 min).
Update lead Google Sheet with latest status.
Thursday
Send WhatsApp Broadcast to warm leads (1 useful tip or product update — keep it short).
Create next week's posts in Canva if not done on Sunday.
Friday
Post Friday content (tips for school principals or education news).
Ask sales team: how many demos happened this week? Any deals closed?
Quick 10-min check: ad budget spent this week vs. plan.
Weekend
Ads still running (automatic). Let leads come in.
Sunday: 30 min to plan next week. Batch-create posts.
Total time per week: 6–8 hours
Daily check-in: 30–45 minutes. Content creation (batched on Sunday): 1.5 hours. Ad monitoring: 1 hour/week. Lead follow-up: 2 hours/week. Sales coordination: 30 minutes/week. This is realistic for one person.
Monthly Tasks (Do Once a Month)
  • Collect 1–2 school testimonials or principal quotes (coordinate with sales)
  • Film or record 1 video (demo walkthrough or school visit)
  • Review ad performance — pause what's not working, increase budget on what is
  • Update management with a simple 5-line performance summary

Honest Caveats
  • Lead quality depends on how quickly the sales team follows up. Digital marketing brings leads — sales team must call within 24 hours for best results.
  • Karnataka government school decisions are often slow. Don't expect all leads to convert within 3 months. Pipeline is valuable too.
  • VR Labs and Robotics Labs need longer sales cycles. Use demo bookings for Smart Classroom as the primary entry point. Others will come up during the conversation.
  • Results will be better if you have existing school testimonials from Day 1. Ask sales team to arrange these before Month 1 starts.
What Success Looks Like
At the end of 3 months: 100+ leads tracked, 35+ demos supported, 5+ active deals with the sales team, and a working digital marketing system (ads, content, WhatsApp follow-up) that runs every week with just 6–8 hours of your time.
What NOT to Do

This is as important as the plan itself. These mistakes will waste your time and budget. Avoid them completely in the first 3 months.

✕
Do not target all of India Karnataka schools are your buyers. A principal in Bihar has no use for your Kannada content or your sales team's visit. Targeting India wastes 80% of your budget on wrong people.
✕
Do not run 5 campaigns at once More campaigns = split budget = no campaign performs well. Run 1 Meta campaign + 1 Google campaign. That's it. Master them first.
✕
Do not post only product promotion If every post says "Buy our Smart Classroom", principals will ignore you. Mix product posts with school tips, success stories, and education news. Ratio: 1 product post for every 2 value posts.
✕
Do not ignore leads for more than 24 hours Lead goes cold very fast in B2B. If you respond after 48 hours, the principal has already forgotten why they filled the form. Respond on the same day — ideally within 2 hours via WhatsApp.
✕
Do not build a website or SEO in Month 1 SEO takes 6–12 months to show results. You need leads NOW. Focus on paid ads and WhatsApp for 3 months. Website and SEO are for Month 4 onwards.
✕
Do not use LinkedIn as a primary channel Karnataka school owners and principals are NOT on LinkedIn. They are on Facebook and WhatsApp. Put zero budget in LinkedIn for these 3 months.
✕
Do not create content for all 7 products at once You have Smart Classroom, VR Labs, Robotics, Content, Chinnara Pettige, Hardware, Smart Books. Pick 1–2 products for Months 1 and 2. Smart Classroom is the easiest to understand and buy. Start there. Add others in Month 3.
✕
Do not send bulk WhatsApp to cold numbers Cold bulk WhatsApp blasts will get your number banned. Send WhatsApp only to people who have shown interest (clicked your ad, called you, visited a demo). For cold outreach, use Facebook ads only.
✕
Do not use English-only creatives Kannada language ads consistently outperform English in Karnataka for school audiences. If you only use English, your cost per lead will be 2–3x higher. Always make a Kannada version.
✕
Do not try to do everything yourself without sales team input Sales team visits schools every day. They know what principals ask, what objections they raise, what competitors are doing. Use this. Talk to 1–2 sales people every week. It will make your content and ads much better.
The one rule to remember
Do fewer things well. You are one person. 3 good social posts per week, 1 well-targeted Meta campaign, and fast WhatsApp follow-up will outperform a complex strategy that you cannot maintain. Simple and consistent beats complicated and inconsistent — every time.
